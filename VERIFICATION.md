# Verification log

The seed list was checked against official public product pages, download pages, and repositories on 2026-10-03. These are source checks, not claims that every app was installed or purchased. Platform tags are deliberately limited to verified interfaces; consult the vendor for hardware, region, plan, and version requirements.

Each row records the source and the availability evidence used for inclusion. The initial pass establishes a discoverable, available product and documented AI functionality. It does not independently audit vendor adoption claims. Future submissions must additionally supply dated maintenance evidence and a concrete public usage signal under the contribution checklist.

Pricing and license claims are omitted from short entries to avoid implying that a free tier, public repository, or local runtime has no conditions. Reviewers must check those details before accepting new submissions. In particular, self-hostable products can have source-available licenses with restrictions.
