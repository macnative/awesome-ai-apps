# Verification log

The current desktop list was checked against official public product pages, download pages, and repositories on 2026-10-03. The earlier [Mac coverage review](MAC_REVIEW.md) records candidate research; this log contains only entries retained under the desktop-only scope. These are source checks, not claims that every app was installed or purchased. Platform badges are limited to verified desktop applications; consult the vendor for hardware, region, plan, and version requirements.

Each row records the source and the availability evidence used for inclusion. The initial pass establishes a discoverable, available product and documented AI functionality. It does not independently audit vendor adoption claims. New entries from the Mac coverage review include dated maintenance evidence and concrete public usage signals. Future submissions must supply the same evidence under the contribution checklist.

Pricing and license claims are omitted from short entries to avoid implying that a free tier, public repository, or local runtime has no conditions. Reviewers must check those details before accepting new submissions. Desktop apps may use local models or paid cloud providers.
