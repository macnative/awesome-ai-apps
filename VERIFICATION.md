# Verification log

The seed list was checked against official public product pages, download pages, and repositories on 2026-10-03. A subsequent [Mac coverage review](MAC_REVIEW.md) added maintained desktop and command-line tools with linked release and usage evidence. These are source checks, not claims that every app was installed or purchased. Platform tags are deliberately limited to verified interfaces; consult the vendor for hardware, region, plan, and version requirements.

Each row records the source and the availability evidence used for inclusion. The initial pass establishes a discoverable, available product and documented AI functionality. It does not independently audit vendor adoption claims. New entries from the Mac coverage review include dated maintenance evidence and concrete public usage signals. Future submissions must supply the same evidence under the contribution checklist.

Pricing and license claims are omitted from short entries to avoid implying that a free tier, public repository, or local runtime has no conditions. Reviewers must check those details before accepting new submissions. In particular, self-hostable products can have source-available licenses with restrictions.
